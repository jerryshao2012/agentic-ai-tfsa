Meet the TFSA Assistant
A friendly virtual helper that makes Tax-Free Savings Accounts simple. Instead of digging through CRA pages or waiting on hold, customers just ask a question in plain language and get a clear, trustworthy answer — and can even take action right away.

How it helps customers
Answers TFSA questions instantly — in plain, friendly language, any time of day.
Always up to date — it knows the historical rules by heart, and checks official CRA sources in real time when the latest figures are needed.
Tells you exactly how much you can put in — it works out your personal remaining contribution room so you don't risk a penalty.
Lets you act on the spot — it can move money into your TFSA for you, after checking you have both the room and the funds, and gives you a confirmation.
Helps you avoid costly mistakes — it won't let you over-contribute or overdraw, and flags penalty risks before they happen.

Why it's valuable
For customers: faster answers, more confidence, fewer expensive errors, and saving without friction — all in one conversation.
For the bank: handles routine TFSA questions and contributions automatically, freeing staff for complex cases, while staying consistent and accurate.
Built responsibly: it sticks to verified information rather than guessing, checks every transaction for safety, and keeps a reviewable record of how it responds.
